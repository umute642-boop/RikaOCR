# RikaOCR Data Sources, Licensing and Redistribution

This file documents the provenance and redistribution status of third-party data used in RikaOCR.

## Ottoman Turkish Place Names Gazetteer

Source: OttomanNLP / Ottoman Turkish Place Names Gazetteer for NLP
Original size used as source: 44,838 pairs
License reported by the dataset repository: CC BY-NC 4.0

RikaOCR uses this resource for Ottoman Arabic-script -> Latin transliteration experiments.
Users of redistributed copies must comply with the original license and citation requirements.

## Osmanlica.com Rik'a Benchmark

Source: Osmanlica.com Test Datasets
Dataset: Handwritten Ottoman Rik'a OCR/Transliteration Benchmark
Size: 85 pages / 1,575 lines

The provider states that the datasets are supplied for research and academic purposes and asks publications to identify the relevant dataset version and date.

A standard redistribution license has not been verified by the RikaOCR authors at the time of this archive.
Therefore inclusion in this repository does not imply transfer of copyright or broader redistribution rights.
Researchers must also consult the original provider's current terms.

## OpenITI MAKHZAN

Source: OpenITI MAKHZAN
Zenodo DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.19861912

RikaOCR used a selected subset of 3,512 lines for Kraken base-model training.

OpenITI MAKHZAN is publicly distributed through Zenodo. However, the exact redistribution terms applicable to every constituent document have not yet been independently verified for this RikaOCR archive.
Users should consult the original MAKHZAN release, metadata, and rights statements before redistributing source material.

## RikaOCR-generated artifacts

RikaOCR model checkpoints, experiment logs, deterministic split files, metrics, derived selections, source code, and other project-generated artifacts are documented separately from the copyright status of underlying third-party source data.

Publishing a derived model or experiment artifact does not change the license or copyright status of the original source dataset.

## External qualitative test documents

Files under data/external_test/ are retained as research test material.
Their presence in a local research workspace does not automatically establish redistribution permission.
Copyright and archival provenance must be verified before public redistribution.

## General rule

Third-party material remains subject to its original license and copyright terms.
When a redistribution right is unclear, RikaOCR should provide provenance, hashes, download instructions, and processing scripts rather than claiming ownership or unrestricted reuse.
